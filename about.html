Narrative & Retention Approach

Digital video content often gets lost in two extremes: either dry, unengaging recordings or chaotic, over-edited meme montages that distract the viewer. My editorial philosophy focuses on what truly drives results: viewer comprehension, emotional pacing, and Apple-grade visual polish.

Whether crafting a long-form YouTube documentary, cutting viral high-retention reels, or editing an intimate multi-camera podcast, every cut, sound cue, and motion graphic exists to serve the story and sustain retention from the first second to the final frame.
